# CC-MATH 0.1 — Counterfactual Draft

**Not presently operative. Not legal advice. Not affiliated with or endorsed by
Creative Commons.** This text records a proposed public-good dedication. Gay.jl
remains under the terms stated in `LICENSE` unless and until every necessary
rights holder deliberately adopts a final version.

## Gay color key

The colors annotate what each clause does; they do not identify a right,
person, work, or authority. The trit is read from the clause's operation:

- **🟥 +1 PLUS — `#ff6464` — Play:** opens a use or creates a permission.
- **🟩 0 ERGODIC — `#64c864` — Witness:** preserves common ground or records a
  boundary without adding a restriction.
- **🟦 −1 MINUS — `#6464ff` — Coplay:** rejects capture, tests adoption, or
  limits what the dedication can honestly claim.

These are Gay.jl contextual presentation tiles. The words and trits carry the
audit meaning; RGB does not. A balanced palette is not evidence that the legal
claims are correct, effective, or complete.

## 🟩 0 ERGODIC — The mathematical commons

Mathematics is discovered, practiced, checked, taught, and extended in common.
When software is mathematics made executable, permission should not stand
between a person and the ability to use it. Like penicillin, a mathematical
instrument realizes its fullest value when every person may examine it,
produce it, improve it, and put it to any necessary use.

Identity belongs to typed referents. Representations belong to tiles,
artifacts, and interfaces. Neither a notation nor an implementation owns the
mathematical identity it represents.

No field of mathematics, science, art, medicine, education, government, or
commerce is excluded. No source-disclosure, share-alike, noncommercial,
no-derivatives, or attribution condition is imposed.

## 🟥 +1 PLUS — Patent non-assertion

To the extent a contributor controls patent claims necessarily infringed by
that contributor's contribution alone, the contributor irrevocably promises
not to assert those claims against any person for making, having made, using,
selling, offering, importing, modifying, or distributing the contribution.
This promise does not claim to dispose of patents controlled by anyone else.

## 🟦 −1 MINUS — No capture of mathematics

CC-MATH grants rights; it does not manufacture exclusive rights in facts,
ideas, theorems, proofs as abstract reasoning, algorithms, identities,
relations, structures, or other matter that law already leaves free. Nothing
in this text should be read as conceding that such mathematical subject matter
is owned or ownable.

Outputs do not become encumbered merely because they were computed with a
CC-MATH work. A person's independent inputs, observations, identities, and
results remain theirs or remain free according to the law that already applies.

## 🟦 −1 MINUS — Rights this dedication cannot erase

This dedication applies only to rights controlled by the contributor who
applies it. It does not override a third party's copyright, patent, trademark,
privacy, publicity, confidentiality, data-protection, export-control, or other
rights. A distributor must identify separately licensed components rather than
pretend that CC-MATH has erased their terms.

Names and marks are not licensed to imply endorsement or origin. Describing
the actual source of a work remains permitted.

## 🟩 0 ERGODIC — Outputs remain with their referents

The license colors are representations of clauses, not a means of acquiring
identity or authority. Copying a red, green, or blue tile does not copy a
person, observation, source, theorem, capability, or ownership claim. A typed
referent remains the identity-bearing object; tiles, artifacts, and interfaces
remain presentations of it.

## 🟦 −1 MINUS — Counterfactual adoption test

A final CC-MATH should be adopted only after:

1. the complete rights and dependency inventory is public;
2. every necessary rights holder has affirmatively applied it;
3. incompatible third-party material is removed or clearly separated;
4. the waiver, fallback license, patent promise, and disclaimer receive legal
   review in the jurisdictions that matter; and
5. a stable name and machine-readable identifier are chosen without implying
   Creative Commons sponsorship.

Until then, this is a proposition about the fairest treatment of executable
mathematics—not a replacement for the repository's effective license.
