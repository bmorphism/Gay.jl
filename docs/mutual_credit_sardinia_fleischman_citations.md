## CORE PAPERS (by Fleischman et al.)

### P1. Fleischman, Dini, Littera (2020)
**"Liquidity-Saving through Obligation-Clearing and Mutual Credit: An Effective Monetary Innovation for SMEs in Times of Crisis"**
- Journal: J. Risk Financial Manag. 13(12):295
- DOI: 10.3390/jrfm13120295
- Authors: Tomaž Fleischman (Be Solutions / Cycles Protocol SA), Paolo Dini (LSE / U. Hertfordshire), Giuseppe Littera (Sardex S.p.A.)
- Key finding: Obligation-clearing alone reduces net internal debt by ~25%; combined with mutual credit (Sardex), ~50%.
- Data: 3,199 Sardex firms, 138,378 transactions, EUR 31M (2019 B2B)
- Semantic Scholar: 19 citations, 30 references

### P2. Fleischman & Dini (2021)
**"Mathematical Foundations for Balancing the Payment System in the Trade Credit Market"**
- Journal: J. Risk Financial Manag. 14(9):452
- DOI: 10.3390/jrfm14090452
- Authors: Tomaž Fleischman (BeSolutions), Paolo Dini (LSE / U. Hertfordshire)
- Key contribution: (1) Liquidity node with store-of-value in obligation-clearing; (2) Proof that one or more liquidity sources can be solved with same machinery as without; (3) Maximum aggregate debt reduction guaranteed via Minimum Cost Flow
- Based on: TCT (Tetris Core Technologies) — running in Slovenia since 1991

### P3. Buchman, Dini, Ahmed, Miller, Fleischman (2024/2025)
**"Cycles Protocol: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Clearing System"**
- arXiv: 2507.22309v1 [cs.CE], July 2025 (v0.5, November 2024)
- Authors: Ethan Buchman (Cosmos/Tendermint), Paolo Dini, Shoaib Ahmed, Andrew Miller, Tomaž Fleischman
- Key innovation: Open decentralized clearing + settlement + issuance. Privacy-preserving multilateral settlement via graph optimization. System of Intents (Obligations + Acceptances + Tenders). Integration of crypto/stablecoins/mutual credit as liquidity sources.
- Affiliated: Cycles Protocol SA (cycles.money), Informal Systems
- 46 references
