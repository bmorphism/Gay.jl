## Vibe Snipe Interpretation

**"Vibe snipe"** = precisely targeting and capturing a specific aesthetic/energy using chromatic trajectories.

In the Gay.jl framework:
- **Target vibe** = a specific RGB color (e.g., #FFFF69)
- **Snipe** = use entropy-guided random walk to hit target with minimal steps
- **Vibe snipe distance** = minimum Euclidean distance achieved during trajectory
- **Snipe quality** = accuracy × efficiency (early hits score higher)
